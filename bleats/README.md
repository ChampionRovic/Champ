# Bleat bank

Real bleats from Donnie's goats, cut from the farm videos and confirmed by ear by Donnie. One goes at the top of every post. Rotate them.
Every clip is loudness-matched (-18 LUFS, -1 dBTP peak). Earshot ranks all of them as strong matches for "a baby goat bleating".
